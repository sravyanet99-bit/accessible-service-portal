    Accessibility

    This service is designed to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA and Section 508. It works with a keyboard alone, with screen readers, at 400% zoom, and in high-contrast modes.

    • Tab and Shift + Tab move between controls.
    • Arrow keys move between priority options.
    • Enter or Space activate buttons, including column sorting.
    • Escape closes a dialog and returns you to where you were.

    Found a barrier? Submit a request in the "Accessibility barrier" category and we will respond within two business days.
